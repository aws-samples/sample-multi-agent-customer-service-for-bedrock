*Part of AnyCompany Utilities. Customer billing reference guide for service agents and the

## 1. Bills & charges

### Q1. How is my AnyCompany Energy bill calculated?
Your bill has two components:
1. **Standing charge** — a fixed daily amount that covers the cost of connecting you to
   the network, maintaining pipes and the meter, and 24/7 supply readiness. It is charged
   every day regardless of how much you use.
2. **Unit charges** — the amount of energy or water you actually consumed, measured in
   kilowatt-hours (kWh) for energy or cubic metres (m³) for water, multiplied by the unit
   rate for your tariff.

Your total = (standing charge × number of days in the period) + (units used × unit rate)
+ VAT at the domestic rate of 5%.

### Q2. How often will I be billed?
Most domestic customers receive a bill **every quarter (every three months)**. Customers
who pay by monthly Direct Debit receive a statement quarterly but pay a smoothed fixed
amount each month. Smart-meter customers can opt into **monthly billing** based on actual
reads.

### Q3. What is a standing charge and can I avoid it?
The standing charge is a fixed daily fee (see `tariff-guide.md` for the exact pence-per-day
on your tariff). It applies even if you use no energy at all, because it pays for network
maintenance and meter servicing. It cannot be removed while you hold an active supply
contract, but it does differ between tariffs.

### Q4. Why has my bill gone up compared with last quarter?
Common reasons include: higher seasonal usage (heating in winter), a tariff price change at
your renewal, a move from estimated to actual meter reads (a "catch-up" bill), the addition
of a new appliance such as an EV charger, or arrears from a previous period being carried
forward. If none of these apply, ask an agent to review your reads for a possible billing
error.

### Q5. What does "estimated" mean on my bill?
If we do not have an actual meter reading for the billing period, we estimate your usage
based on your historical consumption. Estimated reads are marked with an **"E"** next to the
figure. Actual reads are marked **"A"** (customer-supplied) or **"M"** (taken by our meter
reader). Submitting an actual read will correct any over- or under-estimate.

### Q6. What is VAT charged at on my bill?
Domestic energy and water supply is charged at the reduced VAT rate of **5%**. Business and
commercial supplies may be charged at the standard rate; those accounts are handled by our
business team.

### Q7. I think my bill is wrong — what should I do?
First, take and submit a current meter reading so we can re-bill on actual usage. If you
still believe the charge is incorrect, ask an agent to raise a **billing dispute**. While a
dispute is open we will not pursue the disputed amount through collections. See the disputes
and complaints process in `policies.md`.

### Q8. Why was I charged a late-payment or default fee?
A reasonable administration charge may apply where a payment is missed and no payment
arrangement is in place. We always attempt to contact you first. If you are struggling to
pay, contact us **before** the due date to set up a payment plan and avoid charges — see
`policies.md`.

## 2. Payments & Direct Debit

### Q9. What ways can I pay my bill?
- **Monthly Direct Debit** (our recommended method — usually the cheapest)
- **Quarterly Direct Debit** (on receipt of each bill)
- Debit or credit card online or by phone
- Bank transfer / standing order using the reference on your bill
- At a PayPoint outlet using your payment card (prepayment and on-demand customers)

### Q10. How is my monthly Direct Debit amount worked out?
We estimate your total annual cost based on your tariff and expected usage, then divide by
12 so you pay the same amount each month. This smooths winter peaks. We review your Direct
Debit at least once a year, and we will tell you before any change.

### Q11. Why has my Direct Debit been recalculated?
Direct Debit reviews happen when your actual usage differs from our estimate, your tariff
price changes, or your account builds up a large credit or debit balance. If your account is
in credit we may reduce your payments; if it is in debit we may increase them to clear the
shortfall over the coming year.

### Q12. Can I change my Direct Debit payment date?
Yes. We can move your collection date to any working day of the month that suits your pay
cycle. Allow up to 10 working days for the change to take effect; the next collection may
fall on the old date if the change is requested too close to it.

### Q13. What happens if my Direct Debit payment fails (bounces)?
We will attempt to re-present the payment, normally within 5–7 working days, and write to
you. A failed Direct Debit does not immediately put you in arrears, but repeated failures
may. If your account holder is **James Okafor (ACC-100456)** style — i.e. already on a
payment plan — a failed payment should be flagged to the collections team so the plan can be
reviewed rather than defaulted.

### Q14. How do I set up or cancel a Direct Debit?
You can set one up online, in the app, or by phone — we need your account number, sort code
and account number, and confirmation you are the account holder. To cancel, contact your
bank, but please tell us first so we can agree an alternative payment method and avoid your
account falling into arrears.

### Q15. I have a credit balance — can I get it back?
Yes. If your account is in credit you can request a refund (see Q18) or leave the credit on
the account to offset future bills. We may retain a small buffer if you pay by Direct Debit
and winter is approaching, to avoid a large payment increase.

## 3. Meter readings

### Q16. How and when should I submit a meter reading?
You can submit a reading any time online, via the app, or by phone. We recommend submitting
a read **at least once a quarter**, ideally just before your bill date, so we bill on actual
rather than estimated usage. Smart-meter customers do not need to submit reads — these are
sent automatically. See `service-procedures.md` for our meter-reader visit cadence.

### Q17. How do I read an Economy 7 (two-rate) meter?
An Economy 7 meter has two registers: **Rate 1 (or "Low"/"Night")** for the cheaper
overnight units and **Rate 2 (or "Normal"/"Day")** for daytime units. Read both registers
and submit both numbers. Customers like **Sarah Thompson (ACC-100123)** on Economy 7 should
always give both reads, or the night discount cannot be applied correctly.

### Q18. My meter reading looks far higher/lower than usual — what now?
Double-check you have read the correct register and ignored any digits after a decimal point
or in red. If the read is genuinely unusual, an agent can review your consumption history. A
sudden spike with no change in usage may indicate a faulty meter or, for water, an
underground leak — see Q26 and `service-procedures.md` (LEAK_INVESTIGATION).

### Q19. How often does AnyCompany take its own meter readings?
We aim to obtain an actual reading **at least once every 12 months** for safety and accuracy,
and typically attempt a read each quarter for non-smart meters. If we cannot gain access we
will leave a card and may arrange an appointment.

### Q20. My meter is hard to access or in a locked area — what can I do?
Tell us and we will note the access requirements on your account. You can submit your own
reads to avoid estimated bills, or book a **METER_READING** appointment for a time when you
can provide access. See `service-procedures.md`.

## 4. Refunds & credit

### Q21. How do I request a refund of credit on my account?
Ask an agent or use self-service. We will confirm your latest meter reading (to ensure the
credit is genuine and not an artefact of estimated billing), then refund to the bank account
or card used for payment. Refunds are typically processed within **10 working days**.

### Q22. Are there any reasons a refund might be declined or delayed?
We may decline or reduce a refund if your account is on estimated reads (we will ask for an
actual read first), if there is an open billing dispute, if you have an arrears balance
elsewhere on linked accounts, or if a winter Direct Debit buffer is being retained to keep
your monthly payments level. We will always explain why.

### Q23. I overpaid by card — can the payment be reversed?
A genuine duplicate or erroneous card payment can usually be reversed to the original card
within a few working days. A payment that simply puts your account in credit is handled as a
credit refund (Q21).

### Q24. Will closing my account trigger an automatic refund?
When you close your account (for example, moving home — see Q25) we produce a **final bill**
using closing meter reads. Any remaining credit after the final bill is refunded
automatically to your registered payment method.

## 5. Moving home

### Q25. I'm moving home — what do I need to do?
Tell us at least **48 hours before** your move date. Provide your move-out date and a closing
meter reading for the old property, plus a forwarding address for your final bill or refund.
If you are staying with AnyCompany Energy at your new address, give us the new address, move-in
date and an opening meter reading so we can open a new account on the right tariff.

### Q26. Who pays for energy/water used on moving day?
The **outgoing occupier** is responsible up to and including the agreed closing read; the
**incoming occupier** is responsible from the opening read. Accurate move-day readings avoid
disputes, so we strongly recommend photographing the meter on the day.

### Q27. Will my tariff move with me?
Not automatically. When you move, we set up a new supply at the new address. You can request
the same tariff (for example **Standard Variable** or **EV Smart**) if it is available at the
new property and the meter type supports it. Tariffs such as **EV Smart** require a smart or
two-rate meter — see `tariff-guide.md`.

### Q28. What if I'm moving into a property already supplied by AnyCompany?
Register as the new occupier with your move-in date and an opening read. We will close the
previous occupier's account and open yours. Until you choose a tariff you will be placed on
our default **Standard Variable** tariff (deemed contract) — you can switch at any time with
no exit fee.

## 6. Smart meters

### Q29. What is a smart meter and what are the benefits?
A smart meter records your usage automatically and sends reads to us securely over a dedicated
network, so your bills are based on actual usage — no estimates and no manual reads. It also
enables an in-home display showing near-real-time usage and cost, and unlocks time-of-use
tariffs such as **EV Smart**.

### Q30. Does a smart meter cost extra?
There is no separate up-front charge for a standard smart-meter installation; the cost of the
national rollout is recovered through standing charges that apply to all customers. Booking
fees do not apply to a routine smart-meter exchange.

### Q31. How do I get a smart meter installed?
Book an installation appointment (an **ENGINEER_VISIT** type for the smart-meter exchange).
An engineer attends, isolates the supply briefly, swaps the meter and commissions it. Typical
duration and access requirements are described in `service-procedures.md`.

### Q32. My smart meter has gone "dumb" / stopped sending reads — what now?
Occasionally a smart meter loses its network connection (for example after a home-area-network
fault or a SIM issue). It still records usage correctly. Submit a manual read in the meantime
and report the loss of connectivity; if it does not self-recover we will book an engineer to
re-commission it.

### Q33. Can I switch to a time-of-use tariff like EV Smart with a smart meter?
Yes — a smart meter (or a compatible two-rate meter) is required for **EV Smart** so we can
bill the overnight EV charging window separately. Customers such as **Priya Patel
(ACC-100789)** on EV Smart benefit from a low off-peak rate for charging an electric vehicle.
See `tariff-guide.md` for the off-peak window and rates.

### Q34. Is my smart-meter data private?
Yes. We collect the level of read frequency needed to bill you and operate your tariff. You
can choose how often we collect detailed data (e.g. half-hourly, daily, or monthly) except
where half-hourly data is required to operate a time-of-use tariff you have chosen. Your data
is handled in line with UK data-protection law.
