# AnyCompany Energy — Tariff Guide (2026/27)

*Part of AnyCompany Utilities. This guide describes the domestic tariffs offered by AnyCompany
Energy, the unit rates and standing charges that apply, who each tariff suits, and how to
switch. All prices are illustrative for the 2026/27 charging year, shown in GBP, and exclude
VAT at the domestic rate of 5% unless stated. Prices are subject to the regulated price cap
where applicable.*

> **Consistency note.** The tariffs documented here are the only domestic tariffs the
> AnyCompany customer-service agents will quote: **Economy 7**, **Standard Variable**, and
> **EV Smart** (plus the legacy **Fixed Saver** for reference). These match the tariffs held
> on the sample demo accounts (see the comparison table).

*Off-peak windows are local clock time and adjust automatically for British Summer Time. The
EV Smart off-peak window is deliberately tighter and cheaper than Economy 7's night rate
because it is optimised for overnight electric-vehicle charging.*

## 2. Economy 7

**Best for:** households that can shift a meaningful share of usage to the night — for
example electric storage heating, immersion hot-water tanks, dishwashers and washing machines
run overnight.

**How it works.** Economy 7 gives you **seven cheaper hours overnight** (00:30–07:30) on a
separate meter register, and a higher rate for the rest of the day. Your meter records two
readings:
- **Rate 1 / Night / Low** — units used during the off-peak window.
- **Rate 2 / Day / Normal** — units used the rest of the time.

Both registers must be read and submitted each billing period, otherwise the night discount
cannot be applied correctly.

**Indicative charges (2026/27):**
- Standing charge: **53.0 p/day**
- Day unit rate: **28.6 p/kWh**
- Night unit rate: **14.2 p/kWh** (00:30–07:30)

**Worth knowing.** The daytime rate on Economy 7 is *higher* than the Standard Variable
single rate, so Economy 7 only saves money if you genuinely use a large portion (commonly
cited as roughly 40% or more) of your energy at night. Households with mostly daytime usage
are usually better off on Standard Variable.

**Sample account.** *Sarah Thompson (ACC-100123)* is on Economy 7. She should always submit
both meter registers — see `billing-faqs.md` Q17.

## 3. Standard Variable

**Best for:** customers who want simplicity and flexibility with no fixed term and no exit
fees, and who do not have predictable night-time or EV usage. This is also our **default
("deemed") tariff** for new occupiers who have not yet chosen a tariff.

**How it works.** A single unit rate applies at all times of day. The rate can vary in line
with wholesale costs and the regulated price cap; we give advance notice of any change.

**Indicative charges (2026/27):**
- Standing charge: **53.0 p/day**
- Unit rate: **24.9 p/kWh** (single rate, all hours)

**Worth knowing.** There is **no exit fee** and **no fixed end date**, so you can switch to
another AnyCompany tariff at any time. Because the rate can move, your monthly Direct Debit may
be reviewed when prices change (see `billing-faqs.md` Q11).

**Sample account.** *James Okafor (ACC-100456)* is on Standard Variable and is on a payment
plan; his balance is clear.

## 4. EV Smart

**Best for:** households with an electric vehicle and a smart meter who can schedule charging
overnight. The very low off-peak rate is designed to make home EV charging as cheap as
possible.

**How it works.** EV Smart is a **time-of-use tariff**. You get an ultra-low **EV off-peak
rate (00:00–05:00)** intended for charging your vehicle and running other deferrable loads,
and a standard rate at all other times. A **smart meter is required** so we can measure
half-hourly usage and bill the off-peak window separately.

**Indicative charges (2026/27):**
- Standing charge: **55.0 p/day** (slightly higher than single-rate tariffs)
- Standard (peak) unit rate: **27.4 p/kWh**
- EV off-peak unit rate: **7.5 p/kWh** (00:00–05:00)

**Worth knowing.** The off-peak window (5 hours) is shorter than Economy 7's night window (7
hours) but the rate is far lower. To get the best value, schedule your EV and high-load
appliances to run inside the 00:00–05:00 window using your charger's timer or the vehicle's
schedule. If you install or upgrade a home charger you may need an **EV_CHARGER_INSTALL**
appointment — see `service-procedures.md`.

**Sample account.** *Priya Patel (ACC-100789)* is on EV Smart, is currently in arrears, and
is on the Priority Services Register (a vulnerable customer). Arrears handling and PSR
protections are described in `policies.md`.

## 5. Fixed Saver (legacy — closed to new sign-ups)

**Best for:** customers who want price certainty for 12 months. This tariff is shown for
reference and customer continuity; it is **closed to new sign-ups** but existing customers
may remain on it until renewal.

**How it works.** A single unit rate is **fixed for 12 months** from sign-up, protecting you
from in-term price changes. An **early-exit fee** may apply if you leave before the end of the
term.

**Indicative charges (2026/27):**
- Standing charge: **51.0 p/day**
- Unit rate: **25.4 p/kWh** (fixed for 12 months)

**Worth knowing.** Because it is fixed, your rate will not fall if wholesale prices drop
during the term. At renewal you will be moved to **Standard Variable** unless you choose
another available tariff.

## 6. Comparison summary — which tariff suits whom?

| If you... | Recommended tariff |
|---|---|
| Use a lot of energy overnight (storage heaters, immersion) | **Economy 7** |
| Want simplicity, flexibility, no fixed term, no exit fee | **Standard Variable** |
| Own an EV and have a smart meter, can charge overnight | **EV Smart** |
| Want a price fixed for 12 months (existing customers only) | **Fixed Saver** (legacy) |
| Are a new occupier and haven't chosen yet | **Standard Variable** (default deemed contract) |

## 7. How to switch tariff

1. **Check eligibility.** Some tariffs need a specific meter: **EV Smart** requires a smart
   meter, and **Economy 7** requires a two-rate or smart meter. If you need a meter exchange,
   we will book an **ENGINEER_VISIT** (see `service-procedures.md`).
2. **Request the switch.** Ask an agent, use the app, or self-serve online. We confirm the new
   tariff's standing charge and unit rate(s) before you commit.
3. **Provide a meter reading.** A current read on the switch date ensures your old tariff is
   closed and the new one opened on accurate figures.
4. **Cooling-off period.** Domestic switches include a **14-day cooling-off period** during
   which you can cancel without penalty.
5. **No exit fees** apply to switches *from* Standard Variable, Economy 7 or EV Smart. The
   legacy **Fixed Saver** may carry an early-exit fee if you leave before its 12-month term
   ends.
6. **Confirmation.** We send written confirmation of your new tariff, the effective date, and
   your first Direct Debit amount if it changes (see `billing-faqs.md` Q10–Q11).

> **Agent guidance.** When recommending a tariff, always confirm the customer's meter type
> first, then base the recommendation on their usage pattern (daytime vs night-time vs EV
> charging). Never quote a rate not listed in this guide.
