# AnyCompany Energy — Service & Engineer Visit Procedures

*Part of AnyCompany Utilities. This document describes how AnyCompany Energy schedules and carries out
field visits, the appointment types we offer, what each involves, how to prepare, access
requirements, typical durations and time windows, and our cancellation/reschedule rules. It
is the authoritative source for the scheduling assistant.*

> **Consistency note.** The four appointment types below are the only types the scheduling
> agent will book: **METER_READING**, **ENGINEER_VISIT**, **LEAK_INVESTIGATION**,
> **EV_CHARGER_INSTALL**. These align with `policies.md`, `tariff-guide.md` and
> `billing-faqs.md`. Tariff-related visits (e.g. a meter exchange to enable EV Smart or
> Economy 7) are booked as ENGINEER_VISIT.

---

## 1. How appointments work

### 1.1 Time windows
We book field visits into one of four standard two-hour **arrival windows** on a chosen date:
- **08:00–10:00**
- **10:00–12:00**
- **13:00–15:00**
- **15:00–17:00**

The window is the time the engineer will **arrive**; longer jobs (such as an EV charger
install or a large engineer visit) may then take several hours on site beyond the arrival
window. When you check availability we offer two windows per day across the next three
available days from your preferred date.

We send a confirmation with your **booking reference** (format `APPT-` followed by 6 digits),
the date, the arrival window and the appointment type. On the day, the engineer aims to call or
text ahead. PSR customers (see `policies.md`) receive priority notification and may set a
door-step password to verify the engineer's identity.

### 1.2 What you must provide
For every visit:
- **Safe access** to the meter, stop tap, or work area for the full time window.
- An adult (18+) present where the work requires entry to the property or could interrupt
  supply.
- Any pets secured, and a clear, safe working space.

### 1.3 Booking, rescheduling and cancellation
- **Book** by giving us your account number, the appointment type, your postcode and (if
  applicable) a preferred date; we then offer available slots. To confirm, we need a
  **contact phone number**.
- **Reschedule or cancel** free of charge up to **24 hours before** the start of the time
  window, using your booking reference.
- **Late cancellation / missed appointment:** if you cancel with less than 24 hours' notice,
  or no one provides access during the window ("no access"), a **missed-appointment charge**
  may apply for chargeable visit types (this charge is waived for PSR/vulnerable customers and
  for safety-related visits such as leaks).
- Emergencies (no water, major leak, gas-like smell from equipment, danger to life) are
  handled as **priority** and do not require a standard appointment — call our emergency line
  and we attend as fast as possible.

## 2. Appointment types

### 2.1 METER_READING
**Purpose.** A AnyCompany meter reader attends to take an actual reading where we have been
unable to obtain one remotely or where access has repeatedly been unavailable (see
`policies.md` Section 4 and `billing-faqs.md` Q19–Q20).

**What it involves.** The reader records the meter register(s) — both Rate 1/Night and Rate
2/Day for an Economy 7 two-rate meter (e.g. *Sarah Thompson, ACC-100123*) — and checks the
meter for obvious faults or tampering. No supply interruption.

**Preparation.** Clear access to the meter; if it is in a cupboard, garage or external box,
ensure it is unlocked and reachable.

**Access requirements.** Brief access to the meter location only; an adult should be present
if the meter is indoors.

**Typical duration.** 10–20 minutes.

**Typical arrival window.** Any of the four windows (e.g. 08:00–10:00 or 13:00–15:00).

---

### 2.2 ENGINEER_VISIT
**Purpose.** A qualified engineer attends for meter work and general supply tasks, including
**smart-meter installation/exchange**, replacing a faulty meter, fitting a two-rate meter to
enable **Economy 7**, fault investigation, safety inspections, and prepayment-meter
installation where agreed (see `policies.md` Section 2.2).

**What it involves.** The engineer may briefly isolate the supply to work safely, swap or
commission the meter, test it, and (for smart meters) connect the in-home display and confirm
the meter is sending reads. They will explain anything you need to know before leaving.

**Preparation.** Clear the space around the meter; ensure an adult (18+) is present for the
whole window because the supply may be interrupted. If the visit is to enable a new tariff,
have your account details to hand.

**Access requirements.** Full access to the meter and a power source; the supply may be off
for short periods.

**Typical duration.** 60–120 minutes (a smart-meter exchange is typically around 90 minutes).

**Typical arrival window.** Any of the four windows; an earlier window (08:00–10:00 or
10:00–12:00) is preferable for longer jobs so the work completes the same day.

---

### 2.3 LEAK_INVESTIGATION
**Purpose.** A water-network technician investigates a suspected leak — for example after an
unexplained spike in metered usage (see `billing-faqs.md` Q18), visible water, a drop in
pressure, or a customer report. This is treated as a **priority/safety** visit.

**What it involves.** The technician locates the leak (internal plumbing, supply pipe, or
network main), uses leak-detection equipment, advises on responsibility (customer-side vs
network-side), and either makes a temporary safe-and-secure fix or raises follow-on repair
work. Customer-side findings come with advice and, where eligible, signposting to our leak
allowance/repair support.

**Preparation.** Provide access to the internal stop tap and the property; note where you
have seen water or damp. Keep children and pets clear of any standing water.

**Access requirements.** Access to internal and external stop taps and the suspected leak
area; an adult should be present.

**Typical duration.** 45–120 minutes depending on how easily the leak is located.

**Typical arrival window.** Any of the four windows; emergencies are attended as priority
outside standard windows. Missed-appointment charges are waived for leak investigations.

---

### 2.4 EV_CHARGER_INSTALL
**Purpose.** Installation or upgrade of a home electric-vehicle charge point, and any
associated metering work needed to support the **EV Smart** time-of-use tariff (see
`tariff-guide.md`). Relevant to EV customers such as *Priya Patel (ACC-100789)*.

**What it involves.** A qualified installer surveys the supply, confirms the consumer unit and
earthing arrangements are suitable, installs and tests the charge point, and (where needed)
arranges a **smart-meter exchange** so off-peak EV charging (00:00–05:00) can be billed at the
EV Smart off-peak rate. The installer demonstrates how to schedule charging within the
off-peak window. Notification of the new charge point is registered with the network operator
as required.

**Preparation.** Decide on the charger location (typically near the parking space and the
consumer unit), ensure clear access to the consumer unit/fuse board, and have an adult (18+)
present for the full window. A pre-install survey may be booked first for complex properties.

**Access requirements.** Access to the consumer unit, the external wall/parking area, and the
electricity meter; the supply may be isolated briefly.

**Typical duration.** 3–4 hours on site; a separate survey, if required, is shorter (around
30–45 minutes).

**Typical arrival window.** An early window — **08:00–10:00** is recommended so the 3–4 hour
installation completes the same day.

## 3. Appointment quick reference

All visits are booked into one of four two-hour arrival windows: **08:00–10:00**,
**10:00–12:00**, **13:00–15:00**, **15:00–17:00**.

| Type | Purpose | Supply interrupted? | Typical duration | Suggested arrival window | Adult required |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| METER_READING | Take an actual read | No | 10–20 min | Any of the four | If meter indoors |
| ENGINEER_VISIT | Meter work / smart-meter / faults | Briefly | 60–120 min | Any (earlier for long jobs) | Yes |
| LEAK_INVESTIGATION | Find & make-safe a leak | Possibly | 45–120 min | Any (priority if urgent) | Yes |
| EV_CHARGER_INSTALL | Install/upgrade EV charge point | Briefly | 3–4 hours | Early (08:00–10:00) | Yes |

## 4. Agent guidance for scheduling

- Always confirm the **appointment type** from the four valid types and the customer's
  **postcode** before offering slots; capture a **preferred date** if the customer has one.
- To **book**, confirm the chosen **slot id**, the **account number** and a **contact phone**
  number, then read back the booking reference (`APPT-######`), date and window.
- To **cancel or reschedule**, use the booking reference; remind the customer of the 24-hour
  rule and any missed-appointment charge (waived for PSR/vulnerable and leak visits).
- Route a suspected leak, no-water, or any safety concern as **priority** — and, where a
  customer is vulnerable or the matter needs escalation, raise an escalation ticket per
  `policies.md` Section 5.
- For tariff changes that need a meter swap (Economy 7 two-rate meter, or a smart meter for EV
  Smart), book an **ENGINEER_VISIT** and cross-reference `tariff-guide.md`.
